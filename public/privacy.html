Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 • Official Policy for https://mayaai.sbs

01. Introduction

Welcome to MAYA AI (accessible at https://mayaai.sbs, hereinafter referred to as "the Website", "Application", or "Service"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you may provide or create while interacting with MAYA AI.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is processed, the rights you have over your data, and our strict commitment to zero unauthorized sharing or monetization of your personal information.

02. Information We Collect and Process

When you interact with MAYA AI, the application processes the following types of inputs solely to deliver responsive conversational assistance:

  • Text & Conversational Messages: User prompts, queries, and conversational context sent during active sessions.
  • Voice & Microphone Audio: When you activate live voice mode, audio is streamed temporarily to generate speech transcripts. Audio streams are processed ephemerally in real-time and are never stored or archived on our servers.
  • Camera & LiveLens Image Data: When you choose to use the camera feature for skincare analysis or visual diagnosis, image frames are encoded as temporary Base64 representations and processed on-demand. Photos are never uploaded to a persistent photo gallery or stored in a user database.
  • City / Weather Context: When you provide a city name (or request local weather advice), the city query is used to fetch current atmospheric data to personalize skincare tips. We do not track or store persistent background GPS coordinates.
  • Local Browser Storage (LocalStorage): Chat logs, custom nickname/preferences, and contextual conversational memory are stored strictly on your local device via your web browser's localStorage.

03. How Your Information is Used

Your inputs are utilized strictly for the following purposes:

  • Delivering intelligent, contextual AI responses and personalized skincare recommendations.
  • Generating real-time speech responses using Text-to-Speech (TTS) technology.
  • Retaining your conversational continuity on your device via client-side storage.
  • Maintaining security, preventing service abuse, and ensuring app reliability.

Zero Data Selling: We do NOT sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal data, audio, or images to third-party data brokers or marketing firms.

04. Third-Party Service Providers & AI Integrations

To deliver advanced capabilities, MAYA AI securely connects with trusted service providers:

  • Google Gemini API: Artificial Intelligence model inference is executed securely through official Google Gemini APIs via our secure server-side proxy. Data transmitted is encrypted over TLS (Transport Layer Security).
  • WeatherAPI: Used to retrieve live public temperature, humidity, and weather conditions based on user-provided city names.
